Life360 vs Find My comparison questions
Is Life360 better than Find My?
Life360 is better if anyone in your family uses Android or you want crash detection and driving reports, and you're happy to pay for Gold. Find My is better for iPhone-only families who care most about privacy and cost. HeyPolo covers both iPhone and Android with crash alerts included.
What are the main Life360 vs Find My iPhone differences?
Find My is free, Apple only, keeps location for up to 24 hours and has no history. Life360 is cross-platform, keeps up to 30 days of history on Gold and adds crash detection and roadside assistance on paid tiers.
Does Life360 drain more battery than Find My?
Find My is built into iOS and users generally praise its low battery impact. HeyPolo reports location efficiently and refreshes every 5 seconds only during real-time sharing.
Which is more private, Life360 or Apple Find My?
Find My. Apple says it keeps your location for up to 24 hours and doesn't use it for ads. The Markup reported in 2021 that Life360 sold precise location data to data brokers, which it said it would mostly stop in 2022. HeyPolo never sells your location.
Can Life360 and Find My work together?
Yes, they run independently, but you'd be managing two apps. HeyPolo puts iPhone and Android users on one map with one set of alerts.
How much does the Life360 app cost vs Find My?
Find My is free. Life360 is free with limits, then $9.99, $16.99 or $24.99 a month in the US. HeyPolo is $3.99/month billed annually or $11.99 month to month.
