Share Location Between iPhone and Android: Easy Guide
Sharing location between iPhone and Android is harder than it should be. Apple and Android don’t share location with each other natively, so there’s no signal built-in option that works seamlessly. This guide covers the three methods that work across both platforms.

5 min read•June 2, 2026
Can you share the location between an iPhone and an Android?
Yes, you can. The only catch is that Apple and Android don't share location with each other natively, so you won't find a single built-in option that does the job seamlessly.
But there are solutions that work on both  and depending on what you need, some are better suited than others.

2. WhatsApp
If your group is already on WhatsApp, you don't need a separate app. WhatsApp lets you share your live location directly in any chat, one-on-one or in a group.
Keep in mind that location sharing expires after a maximum of 8 hours, so it's better suited for one-off moments than staying connected long-term.
Besides, there are no alerts, no privacy controls, and no way to share a general area instead of your exact spot.
Location sharing instructions on WhatsApp
On iPhone:
Open a chat.
Tap the "+" icon next to the text field on the left.
Select Location.
Tap Share Live Location.
Choose a duration: 15 minutes, 1 hour, or 8 hours.
Tap Send.
On Android:
Open a chat.
Tap the paperclip icon.
Select Location.
Tap Share Live Location.
Choose a duration: 15 minutes, 1 hour, or 8 hours.
Tap Send.
Best for: People who already use WhatsApp daily and need a quick, no-download solution for short-term sharing.
3. Google Maps
Most people already have Google Maps installed. It works across iPhone and Android, and you don't need to download anything new.  just a Google account on both sides.
Before you start, make sure you're signed in to your Google account and that the person you're sharing with is saved in your Google Contacts.
Location sharing instructions on Google Maps
Open Google Maps.
Tap your profile picture in the top right.
Tap Location sharing, then Share location.
Choose how long to share — set a duration or select Until you turn this off.
Select a contact or copy a link to send via text or email.
Tap Share.The recipient will see your location in their Google Maps app in real time.
One thing to know: the person you're sharing with needs a Gmail account to see your location interactively. If they don't have one, they can still view it via a link, but only for up to 24 hours.
Google Maps doesn't have place alerts or privacy controls beyond turning sharing on or off entirely. It's reliable, but it's not built around staying connected with a group long-term.
Best for: Occasional sharing, meeting up, letting someone know you're on the way, or coordinating with someone you don't share location with regularly.
Alternative ways to share location between iPhone and Android
A few other apps let you share location across iPhone and Android, though location sharing is a side feature, not what they're built for (and it shows).
Snapchat lets you share your location on Snap Map, either with all friends or select people. It works across iPhone and Android, but it's tied to the app — if someone doesn't use Snapchat regularly, it's not practical.
Signal lets you share a live location for up to 24 hours in a one-on-one or group chat. Better for privacy than most, but still time-limited and not built for ongoing use.
Facebook Messenger supports live location sharing in one-on-one chats for up to an hour.
None of these apps are designed for staying connected with friends and family day to day. If you need a quick one-off share and already use the app, they'll do the job. Yet none offer place alerts or privacy controls beyond a basic location pin.
Which method is right for you?
All methods work — but they're built for different things.
WhatsApp and Google Maps are convenient if you're already using them and just need a quick, one-time share. Neither is designed for staying connected long-term, and neither gives you much control over who sees what.
HeyPolo is the only one built specifically for ongoing location sharing with privacy controls, place alerts, trip history and an SOS feature.

FAQ
Can you share the location between an iPhone and an Android?
Yes, you can share it, but not with the tools built into your phone. You need an app that works on both. The most convenient options are WhatsApp, Google Maps, and HeyPolo.
Google Maps lets you share continuously, and WhatsApp caps out at 8 hours. Neither gives you much control over who sees what. HeyPolo works across both devices and gives you control over who sees you, how precisely, and when.
Can Android users track iPhone location?
Yes, but only with the right app. Find My is iPhone-only, so it won't work on your Android device. Google Maps, WhatsApp, and HeyPolo all let Android and iPhone users see each other's locations, as long as sharing is enabled.
How to share a live location permanently from an iPhone to an Android?
There are two most convenient options to achieve this. Google Maps and HeyPolo both support ongoing sharing with no set expiry.
In Google Maps, select "Until you turn this off" when prompted. HeyPolo works the same way, but also lets you control visibility person by person, switch between sharing your exact location or just a general area, or share on a recurring schedule.
How do I track my wife's iPhone with my Android?
Location sharing works best when it goes both ways,  both people choose what they share and when.
Google Maps and HeyPolo both work across iPhone and Android. Besides, HeyPolo gives each person control over their own visibility, so it feels like staying connected with your partner rather than being checked on.
Can I share my location with a group of people?
Yes. WhatsApp uses your existing group chats. Google Maps lets you share with multiple contacts at once. HeyPolo is designed specifically for groups and lets you control your visibility separately within each one.
Can you track someone from an iPhone to a Samsung?
Yes, as long as you're using a cross-platform app. Google Maps, WhatsApp, and HeyPolo support iPhone-to-Android tracking, including Samsung. Just note that they all require the other person to share their location with you first.
Is there a free app to share location between iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo, WhatsApp and Google Maps all let you share location across iPhone and Android for free. HeyPolo has a free trial. With one paid HeyPolo subscription, you can stay connected with all of your friends and family.
