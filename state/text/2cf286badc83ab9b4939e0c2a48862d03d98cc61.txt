FindMyKids vs HeyPolo questions
Is FindMyKids free?
There's a free tier with basic location, SOS, chat and two geofences. Listening minutes and fuller features need Premium.
How much does FindMyKids cost per month and per year?
FindMyKids' own blog lists $2.99 a month, $16.99 a year or $25.99 for life. Users report paying more, including $5.99 a month and €46 to €50 a year, so check the price in your store. HeyPolo is $3.99/month billed annually or $11.99 month to month.
Is FindMyKids safe to use?
FindMyKids says it complies with GDPR and COPPA and holds a kidSAFE Seal. Privacy experts have raised concerns about its remote listening feature. HeyPolo has no listening feature and never sells location.
How do I cancel a FindMyKids subscription?
Cancel through the app store you subscribed with; deleting the app doesn't stop billing. Trustpilot reviewers report trouble getting refunds after renewal. HeyPolo comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
What is the difference between FindMyKids and HeyPolo?
FindMyKids is one-way child tracking with GPS watch support and remote listening. HeyPolo is two-way, consent-based sharing for teens and whole families, with crash, SOS and severe weather alerts.
Is HeyPolo better than FindMyKids for teenagers?
We think so. Teens can share a general area or switch sharing on for a set time, and nobody listens in. For young children with a GPS watch, FindMyKids is the better fit.
