Anyone weighing up Life360 free vs paid features is really deciding whether those two limits are worth $9.99 a month. For a lot of families they are not.

Is the upgrade worth it
Free to Silver is the only upgrade that buys you safety
On free, a crash alerts you and your emergency contacts, and whoever sees it first does the phoning. Silver adds a 24/7 dispatcher who calls you, sends an ambulance if you need one, and rings your Circle until somebody answers. With a new driver in the house, that peace of mind is worth $9.99 a month.
Everything above Silver is an assistance bundle. Gold and Platinum add towing, ID theft cover and credit monitoring, and across those two steps only two location features change at all: history goes from 7 days to 30, and place alerts go from 5 to unlimited.
Already have roadside assistance through your car insurer or a motoring club? You are probably paying for it twice on Gold or Platinum.

Got Questions?
Is Life360 free?
Yes, and it stays free. The free plan covers unlimited real-time location sharing for up to 99 people, SOS alerts, crash detection above 25 mph, in-app messaging, a family driving summary, data breach alerts and Tile integration. The limits are 2 days of location history and 2 place alerts.
What is the difference between Life360 free vs paid features?
Free covers real-time location for up to 99 people, SOS alerts, crash detection above 25 mph, in-app messaging, weather warnings and a family driving summary. Paid adds three things on top: more location history, more place alerts, and 24/7 emergency dispatch after a crash or an SOS. Silver at $9.99 takes history from 2 days to 7 and places from 2 to 5, and it is the only step that changes what happens in an emergency. Gold at $16.99 and Platinum at $24.99 add an assistance bundle of towing, ID theft cover and credit monitoring.
What does Life360 Premium add?
Longer location history, more place alerts, and 24/7 emergency dispatch after an SOS or a crash. Silver at $9.99 gives you 7 days of history, 5 places and dispatch. Gold at $16.99 gives you 30 days, unlimited places, roadside assistance, driver reports and ID theft protection. Platinum at $24.99 raises the insurance limits and adds credit monitoring, disaster response and travel support.
Is Life360 Gold worth it?
It depends what you are buying it for. The location upgrade from Silver to Gold is 7 days of history to 30 and 5 place alerts to unlimited. Everything else you are paying for is an assistance bundle: towing, ID theft cover, stolen funds reimbursement. If you already have roadside assistance through your insurer or a motoring club, Gold is worse value than it looks.
Does everyone in my family need to pay for Life360?
No. Life360 memberships cover your whole Circle on one subscription, up to 99 members, on every paid tier. HeyPolo works the same way and covers unlimited members across unlimited groups.
Does Life360 have crash detection on the free plan?
Yes. Free crash detection triggers above 25 mph and alerts you and your emergency contacts. What paid adds is 24/7 emergency dispatch, where a dispatcher calls you, sends emergency services if you need them, and phones your Circle until someone answers.
Does Life360 sell your location data?
Life360 announced in January 2022 that it would stop selling precise location data, after The Markup reported in December 2021 that it had been supplying it to around a dozen brokers. It kept two partnerships, with Arity (owned by Allstate) and Placer.ai, and its own trusted partners page confirms both sharing settings are on by default. The data is described as aggregated. HeyPolo has no data partners, and location data is never sold or shared with advertisers, brokers or insurers.
Can someone check my location on Life360 without me knowing?
Yes. Circle members can view your location and you get no notification. HeyPolo is consent-based and you pick your own precision per person: exact position, general area, or private.
How much does HeyPolo cost compared with Life360?
HeyPolo is one plan at $3.99 a month billed annually ($47.88 upfront), or $5.99 month to month, covering your whole group. Life360's cheapest paid tier is $9.99 a month.
Will I lose anything switching from Life360 to HeyPolo?
Three things. Emergency dispatch, where a 24/7 operator responds to a crash or an SOS and can send an ambulance. The assistance bundle on Gold and Platinum: roadside, towing, ID theft cover and credit monitoring. And a full month of location history, since HeyPolo keeps 7 days rather than 30. Everything else comes across, and precision control and scheduled sharing are things Life360 does not offer on any tier.
How long does switching take?
Usually under ten minutes. Download HeyPolo, create a group, invite the people you share with, set each person's precision, then turn Life360 off once everyone has joined.
