Does the HeyPolo voucher work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo works the same on both, so a mixed-phone household stays on one map and one subscription.
Who does the subscription cover?
One plan covers your whole family. Invited members join free, and the price stays the same whether three people or eight are sharing.
What happens when the first year ends?
Your plan renews at the standard annual price unless you cancel. You can cancel in the app at any point before renewal.
Is this surveillance of my kids?
No. The person sharing decides what they share, with whom, and for how long. They can switch to general-area sharing or turn sharing off, and everyone in the group can see who is sharing with them.
My teenager will just delete it. What then?
That usually happens when an app watches someone without their say. HeyPolo shows each person exactly what their family can see and lets them pause sharing, which is why it tends to survive the first month.
Do you sell our location data?
Never. Not now, not ever. Location is never sold to advertisers, insurers or data brokers, and it is encrypted between your device and your trusted contacts.
Will it drain our phone batteries?
Sharing runs in the background with low-power location updates, and low battery alerts tell your group when someone's phone is close to dying rather than leaving them guessing.
What if one person in the family refuses?
They can stay out of it, or share only during a drive or a night out. Nothing is shared from a phone that has not agreed to share.
How long do you keep our location history?
Location history is kept for seven days, then deleted.
Can I get a refund if it is not for us?
Yes. There is a 30 day money back guarantee, and cancelling takes a few taps in the app.
