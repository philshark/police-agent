1. What is the HeyPolo Service?
A tool designed to provide an additional layer of safety and peace of mind.
‍The HeyPolo Service consists of our informational Website and a personal safety service accessible through our App. The core functionality of the Service utilizes algorithms to analyze location data to learn a user's regular routines, such as frequently visited places and typical hours of activity. Based on this learned behavior, the Service is designed to identify significant deviations from established patterns. When such a deviation is detected, the Service may send a notification to other members of the user's designated group (e.g., family, friends, or colleagues).
The purpose of the Service is to provide informational alerts; it is not a guaranteed safety solution and must not be used as an emergency service. Accessing the core functionality requires creating an account within the App.

When you use these features in a vehicle, you confirm that you are the driver, or that the driver has agreed to this data being collected - if not, you must turn these features off.
Driving Events and Driving Score. The Service detects driving events (such as overspeeding, harsh braking, rapid acceleration, harsh cornering, and distracted driving) and calculates a Driving Score from the number, severity, and duration of these events. The Service attempts to detect automatically whether you were the driver or a passenger, and you can change this manually for a trip. You are responsible for correctly identifying your trips, and we do not guarantee that automatic detection is correct or that changing a trip will fully correct or remove its effect on any score.

Trip Replay. The Service may let group members replay a trip, showing speed and mode of transport along the route.
Crash Detection and Emergency Alerts. The Service may attempt to detect certain vehicle crashes by identifying abnormal acceleration from your device's sensors and, if you do not dismiss the alert within the time provided, notify the members of your group. This feature is not an emergency response service: it does not contact emergency responders, does not dispatch help, and does not replace emergency services.
