SOS alerts FAQ
What does an SOS alert do in HeyPolo?
Tap SOS, then slide to send, and an emergency alert with your live position goes to everyone in your chosen group, even if you usually share only a general area. Everyone is notified at the same time, your position keeps updating every five seconds, and you can see exactly which contacts received it.
How do I send an emergency alert on HeyPolo?
Tap SOS on the map screen, check who the alert will reach, then slide the control across to send it. Your group gets the notification with your live position within seconds. It's worth practicing once with your family, especially with a grandparent or a young teen, because a button you've used before feels a lot less daunting than one you've only read about.
Does HeyPolo work as an SOS app for iPhone and Android?
Yes. Download HeyPolo from the App Store or Google Play, join your family group, and the SOS button is on your map screen from day one. It works exactly the same on both, so an Android SOS app user and an iPhone user can sit in the same group and get the same alerts.
Can I cancel an SOS alert?
Yes. If you've sent an alert and you're fine, cancel it from the SOS screen or let your group know, and everyone who was notified sees the update. If you only tapped SOS and didn't slide, nothing was sent. Close the screen and that's the end of it.
What can my group actually do when they get an alert?
Whatever fits the moment. They can call you, message the group to work out who's nearest, drive over using your live location, ask a neighbor to check on you, or call emergency services and tell them exactly where you are. Because everyone is notified together, nobody assumes someone else has it covered.
Does an SOS app replace calling emergency services?
No. Please call 911 or your local emergency number when someone needs medical, police, or fire response. HeyPolo alerts your family, not dispatchers. The two work well together, since relatives who know where you are can meet responders, unlock a door, or drive over.
Can I use HeyPolo as a personal alarm app for an elderly parent?
Yes. It works as a personal alarm app on Android or iPhone, on the phone your parent already carries, so there's no pendant to remember. Older parents tend to accept it because control stays with them. Private mode lets them share nothing on an ordinary afternoon while the SOS button stays ready, and when they do send an alert, every adult child gets it at once, location included. Low battery alerts help too, flagging a phone that's about to go quiet.
Is HeyPolo a family locator app?
HeyPolo is a location-sharing app, which works differently in practice. Nobody watches anybody. Each person decides who sees them, how precisely, and for how long, and both sides can see that sharing is on. Knowing somebody got home replaces having to ask.
What is the best SOS app for a whole family?
Look at three things: whether the person sharing stays in control, whether every member can send an alert, and what happens to the price as relatives join. HeyPolo leaves control with whoever is sharing, lets everyone you invite send an SOS, and keeps one price however many people you add.
How much battery does it use?
With sharing on in the background, HeyPolo typically uses around 4% of your battery a day, about the same as a messaging app left open. Position refreshes every 5 seconds while you're looking at the map and every 30 seconds when the app is closed, which keeps the drain down.
