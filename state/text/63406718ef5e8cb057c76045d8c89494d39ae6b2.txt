Frequently asked questions
What happens when I send an SOS on the trail?
Everyone in your group gets an SOS alert with your location, so they know you need help and where to find you. It needs a connection and does not contact emergency services. Call the local emergency number when you need emergency help.
Does HeyPolo work when my phone has no signal?
Live location updates and alerts need a connection. Do not rely on them in areas without coverage. Plan your route and agree how you will check in before leaving.
How much battery does HeyPolo use on a long hike?
Battery use depends on your phone, settings and conditions. We do not have a verified day-hike battery figure to quote. Start with a charged phone and bring a power bank if your trip needs one.
Can I share my location for just one hike?
You can choose to share for your outing and pause sharing when you finish. The group can see when sharing is paused. Check the sharing options in your app before setting off.
Can my family see where I went on a hike?
HeyPolo has location sharing history for reviewing recent shared routes. Your sharing choices still matter. Check the history screen and your group settings before your trip.
Do I still need a satellite messenger or personal locator beacon?
HeyPolo relies on a phone connection and does not replace satellite communication equipment or a personal locator beacon. Choose equipment appropriate to your route, coverage and local safety guidance.
Does everyone in my group have to pay?
One person subscribes and everyone they invite joins free. One subscription covers unlimited people and groups, with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
Can iPhone and Android users share in the same group?
Yes. Each person installs HeyPolo on their iPhone or Android phone and joins with the group code.
Is HeyPolo a tracker?
HeyPolo shares your location with the people you choose. Each person agrees to share, controls the level of detail and can pause sharing. The group can see when sharing is paused.
Can I use HeyPolo for a solo run or ride?
Yes. Create a group with someone you trust, so there is always someone to alert if you need help. You can pause sharing when you finish. Live updates need a connection.
How many people can join one group?
As many as you like. You can create multiple groups, and everyone you invite joins free under one subscription.
