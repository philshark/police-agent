Find My Kids app vs Life360 questions
Which is better, Life360 or Find My Kids?
Find My Kids is better for parents of young children with a GPS watch. Life360 is better for families sharing with each other and wanting crash detection, if they pay for Gold. For families with teenagers, HeyPolo covers crash, SOS and weather alerts on one plan without listening in.
How much does Find My Kids cost compared with Life360?
Findmykids lists Premium at $2.99 a month, $16.99 a year or $25.99 for life, though users report paying €46 to €50 a year in Europe. Life360 is $9.99, $16.99 or $24.99 a month in the US. HeyPolo is $3.99/month billed annually.
Can my child tell when I'm listening through Find My Kids?
Findmykids offers a 'sounds around' feature that lets parents hear the child's surroundings, with a few free minutes and paid minutes after that. Privacy experts have called remote audio monitoring intrusive. Neither Life360 nor HeyPolo offers it.
Does Find My Kids have crash detection like Life360?
No. Life360 includes crash detection on Gold and above. HeyPolo includes crash notifications on its single plan.
Is Find My Kids or Life360 better for teenagers?
Teens often push back on one-way tracking and listening features. Life360 is two-way but tiered. HeyPolo is two-way, lets teens share a general area instead of an exact spot, and costs one flat price.
How many people use Find My Kids and Life360?
The Findmykids Google Play listing shows 50M+ downloads. Life360 reported about 102.4 million monthly active users in its Q2 2026 results.
