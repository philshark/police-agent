Apple Find My vs HeyPolo questions
Is HeyPolo better than Apple Find My?
For an all-iPhone family that only needs live location, Find My is free and does the job. HeyPolo vs Apple Find My tips towards HeyPolo when someone uses Android, or when you want general-area sharing, sharing that switches itself off, and SOS, crash and weather alerts that reach your group.
Can I use Find My with someone who has an Android phone?
No. Apple's own community forum says there is no version of Find My for Android. HeyPolo works on iPhone and Android with the same features on both.
Is Apple Find My free?
Yes, in every region, with no iCloud+ needed for sharing with people. The cost shows up when someone in the family doesn't have an Apple device.
Does Find My keep a history of where my family has been?
No. Find My shows a live or last-known location only. HeyPolo keeps seven days of routes and arrival times, then deletes them.
Can I share only my general area in Find My?
No. Find My sharing is on or off for each person. HeyPolo lets you pick exact location, general area or private for each group or person.
Does Find My have crash detection or SOS alerts for family?
iPhone and Apple Watch have Crash Detection and Emergency SOS, which contact emergency services and your emergency contacts. They don't notify the people in your Find My list. HeyPolo sends SOS and crash alerts straight to your group with your location.
Is my location encrypted in Find My?
Apple says live location is encrypted on its servers and kept for up to 24 hours, and that the offline Find My network is end-to-end encrypted. HeyPolo encrypts location with AES-256 at rest and TLS in transit, and never sells it.
How do I move my family from Find My to HeyPolo?
Download HeyPolo, create a group, invite everyone (iPhone and Android), and set each person's precision. You can keep Find My for finding lost devices and AirTags.
