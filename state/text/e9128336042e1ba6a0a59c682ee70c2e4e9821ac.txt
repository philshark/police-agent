Google Maps location sharing vs Life360 questions
Which is better, Life360 or Google Maps?
Google Maps is better for free, casual sharing and web access. Life360 is better for safety features like crash detection, if you pay for Gold. HeyPolo gives you crash, SOS and weather alerts on one plan at $3.99/month billed annually.
Is Google Maps location sharing as accurate as Life360?
Both use your phone's location. The bigger difference in Life360 vs location sharing in Maps is reliability reports: Google Maps users describe sharing silently stopping and locations going stale when updates are switched off. HeyPolo refreshes every 5 seconds during real-time sharing.
Can Google Maps send arrival alerts like Life360?
Yes, up to 5 per person. Life360's free plan allows 2 places, Silver 5, and Gold unlimited. HeyPolo place alerts are unlimited on its one plan.
Does Google Maps have crash detection like Life360?
No. Life360 includes crash detection with emergency dispatch on Gold and above. HeyPolo includes crash notifications to your group on its single plan.
Which is more private, Google Maps or Life360?
It's mixed. Google paid Texas $1.375 billion in 2025 over location tracking claims, and Life360 sold precise location data to brokers until 2022, according to The Markup. HeyPolo never sells your location to advertisers, data brokers or insurers.
How much does Life360 cost compared with Google Maps?
Google Maps location sharing is free. Life360 is free with limits, then $9.99 (Silver), $16.99 (Gold) or $24.99 (Platinum) a month in the US.
