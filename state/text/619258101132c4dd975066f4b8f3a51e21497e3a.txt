How HeyPolo works as a weather alert app for your whole group
HeyPolo pulls from the same public warnings that official meteorological authorities already issue. When one of these agencies puts out a Warning, HeyPolo checks it against where the people in your group actually are, whether that's a precise location or a general area.
A Warning doesn't always mean someone's in immediate danger. Sometimes it means the roads are about to flood, or cell service is about to get patchy, the kind of thing worth knowing even when everyone ends up fine. In extreme cases, it means real danger, and that's exactly when you want to already know.

Weather alerts FAQ
What is a dangerous weather condition app, exactly?
It's an app that tells you when someone you care about is in an area under an official severe weather warning, not just an app that shows you the forecast. HeyPolo checks your group's locations against real public warnings and tells you when there's a match.
What's the best severe weather alert app for a family, not just one phone?
That depends on what your family actually needs, which usually isn't more forecasts. It's fewer false alarms and the right people finding out fast. HeyPolo only triggers on real Warnings, and it tells both the person in the warning zone and their group. Most weather apps built for a single phone don't do the second part.
Is HeyPolo a free weather alert app?
HeyPolo is free to download. Weather alerts come with your HeyPolo subscription, and one plan covers everyone you invite to your family, so the cost doesn't grow as your group does.
Does HeyPolo work as a weather alert app for iPhone?
Yes. HeyPolo is available on iOS, and weather alerts work the same way there as they do on Android.
Does the weather alert app work on Android too?
Yes. iOS and Android get the same warnings, at the same time, with no platform gaps.
What counts as a severe weather warning app alert versus a regular forecast?
A forecast is a prediction. A Warning is an official statement from an agency like the National Weather Service or the UK Met Office that severe conditions are happening or about to. HeyPolo only alerts on the second one.
Is this a code red weather alert app?
Some agencies label their most severe alerts "red," like the UK Met Office's Red Warning. HeyPolo doesn't rename or reinterpret any of this. Whatever the issuing agency calls a Warning is exactly what shows up in your app.
How is HeyPolo different from a severe weather app I already have?
Most severe weather apps are built to tell you about your own location. HeyPolo tells your group about each other's. If your mother is under a tornado warning three states away, she probably knows it already. You might not, until HeyPolo tells you.
