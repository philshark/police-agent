How HeyPolo works as a severe weather alert app for your whole group
HeyPolo uses the official warnings issued by the National Weather Service. When a warning goes out, HeyPolo checks it against where each person in your group is, whether they share an exact location or a general area, and notifies the whole group within minutes.
A warning doesn't always mean immediate danger. Sometimes it means roads are about to flood or cell service is about to get patchy, which is still worth knowing even when everyone ends up fine. When it does mean real danger, you already know.

Weather alerts FAQ
What is the best severe weather alert app for a family?
The best severe weather alert app for a family tells the right people quickly. HeyPolo sends an alert only for official National Weather Service warnings, and it notifies both the person in the warning zone and everyone in their group. Weather apps built for a single phone only tell the person holding it.
Is there a severe weather alert app for both iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo is an iPhone severe weather alert app and a severe weather alert app for Android too. Both get the same warnings at the same time, so a household with mixed phones is covered.
Is HeyPolo a tornado alert app?
Yes. When the National Weather Service issues a Tornado Warning for an area where someone in your group is, everyone in the group is notified within minutes, and the map shows which warning it is and where it reaches.
Which weather warnings does HeyPolo send alerts for?
HeyPolo sends an alert for National Weather Service warnings, including Tornado Warnings, Hurricane Warnings, Flash Flood Warnings, Winter Storm Warnings and Extreme Heat Warnings. The full list is above. Watches and Advisories never trigger an alert.
Where do HeyPolo's weather alerts work?
Weather alerts cover people located in the United States, including Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico, and use National Weather Service warnings. If someone in your group is in the US and in a warning area, everyone in the group is notified, wherever each person is based. HeyPolo sends no weather alerts for locations outside the US.
Is HeyPolo a free severe weather alert app?
HeyPolo is free to download. Weather alerts come with your HeyPolo subscription, from $5.99 a month, and one plan covers everyone you invite, so the cost stays the same as your group grows.
How much does HeyPolo cost, and what does it include?
Plans start from $5.99 a month, and one subscription covers everyone you invite. Weather alerts are included, along with SOS, arrival alerts, general-area sharing and low battery alerts.
Can I use HeyPolo as a severe weather alert app when I travel?
Yes, anywhere in the United States. If a warning covers where you are, the people in your group are notified, and if one covers someone in your group, you are. HeyPolo sends no weather alerts for locations outside the US.
Is there a severe weather alert app for an aging family member?
Yes. If an aging parent is in an area under a warning, you get notified whether or not they call, as long as they share their location with you, either exact or general area. They stay in control: Private Mode turns weather alerts off completely.
How is HeyPolo different from a severe weather app I already have?
Most severe weather apps tell you about your own location. HeyPolo tells your group about each other's. If your mother is under a tornado warning three states away, she has probably seen it already, and you might not until HeyPolo notifies you.
There's more in the app than weather alerts
Every feature is included in the same plan, for everyone in your group.
