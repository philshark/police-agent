HeyPolo vs OtoZen questions
Is HeyPolo better than OtoZen?
For US parents focused on teen driving, OtoZen's in-car alerts and DMV log are genuinely useful. For families outside the US, groups bigger than 8, or anyone who wants general-area sharing and severe weather warnings, HeyPolo is the better fit.
Who makes OtoZen?
Sensovium Inc., a private company based in San Jose, California. OtoZen launched as a driving safety app in November 2022.
Is OtoZen available in the EU?
We couldn't verify it. OtoZen's website lists USD, CAD, GBP, AUD, NZD and INR pricing, and we found no EU store page. HeyPolo is available across the EU.
Does OtoZen crash detection work outside the United States?
No. OtoZen's Help Center says crash detection and professional emergency dispatch are available only within the United States. Outside the US, its SOS notifies only your OtoZen connections.
How much does OtoZen cost?
On otozen.com, Family (up to 4) is $49.99 a year and Family Plus (up to 8) $64.99 a year, shown as 50% off. The UK App Store lists £69.99 and £99.99 a year. HeyPolo is $47.88 a year for unlimited people.
Does OtoZen sell my data?
OtoZen says your data is never sold to third parties. Its Google Play data safety label says the app may share app activity and device IDs with third parties. HeyPolo never sells your location and analytics partners never receive it.
Is OtoZen a Life360 alternative?
It markets itself as one, and some of its store listings use Life360 in the name. HeyPolo is also a Life360 alternative, with one flat plan and no member cap.
