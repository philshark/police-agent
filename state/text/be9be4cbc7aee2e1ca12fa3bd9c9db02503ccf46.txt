Google Maps location sharing vs HeyPolo: your questions
Is HeyPolo better than Google Maps location sharing?
For a one-off ETA, Google Maps is free and fine. HeyPolo is better when you want ongoing family sharing with SOS, crash and severe weather alerts, groups, general-area sharing and unlimited place alerts.
Can Google Maps tell me when my family arrives home?
Yes. When someone shares with you, you can add arrival or departure notifications, up to 5 per person. The person sharing is told. HeyPolo place alerts have no cap.
Does Google Maps location sharing work between iPhone and Android?
Yes, on Android, iPhone and the web. HeyPolo also works on iPhone and Android, though it has no web version.
Does the person I share with need a Google account?
For full sharing, yes. Google Maps Help says the recipient must have a Gmail account; anyone else gets a link that lasts up to 24 hours. HeyPolo only needs the app.
Why does Google Maps location sharing stop working?
Common reports include sharing set to 'until you turn this off' silently stopping, locations going stale when shared-location updates are switched off on Android, and age verification prompts interrupting sharing.
Does Google Maps have crash detection or an SOS button?
No. Some Pixel phones have crash detection, but it doesn't notify the people you share your location with. HeyPolo sends crash and SOS alerts to your group.
What is the difference between Google Maps location sharing, Find Hub and Family Link?
Find Hub (formerly Find My Device) uses the same location sharing service as Google Maps. Family Link is parental supervision for child accounts, with Family places and screen-time controls. HeyPolo is two-way, consent-based sharing for families and friends.
Can I see where someone has been on Google Maps?
No. Timeline only records your own movements, now stored on your device. HeyPolo shows seven days of routes for the people who share with you, then deletes them.
