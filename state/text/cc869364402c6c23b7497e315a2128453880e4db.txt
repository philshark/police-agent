Frequently asked questions
Can I share my location just for walking home?
Yes. Sharing your location when you're walking home is the most common reason people open HeyPolo: set a window when you start, twenty minutes or three hours, and it ends automatically when the window's up. Nothing keeps running afterward.
Do I have to give someone my exact location?
No. General area shows the part of town you're in, which is often enough for a group chat, while one person you trust can have exact location. You set each of those separately.
What happens when I press SOS?
Your exact location goes to your group along with a clear signal that you need help, so they can call you or come to you. It reaches your own people rather than an emergency service, so keep local emergency numbers for what they're for.
Is HeyPolo an emergency contact app?
Keep your local emergency number for anything urgent enough to need one, HeyPolo's SOS works alongside it, not instead of it. One tap sends your exact location to the emergency contacts and groups you've already set up, so whoever comes to help knows where to start without you explaining it over the phone.
Isn't it awkward asking a friend to watch my location?
Less so when it has an end time. 'I've shared until 11' is a small, specific thing to ask, and your friend doesn't have to monitor anything, they just have it if something changes.
Is my location data sold to anyone?
No. It isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. That matters most for exactly these moments, which are among the most sensitive locations anyone shares.
How does HeyPolo compare to other personal safety apps?
It depends what you're optimizing for. HeyPolo skips the tiers and data trade-offs that come with some of the bigger names: one flat price covers everyone you invite, and your location is never sold. For a side-by-side, the comparison pages below cover HeyPolo against Find My Friends, GeoZilla, and Life360 directly.
Does the app drain my battery on a long night out?
Updates only go out while you're sharing, and sharing windows are usually short, which keeps use modest. Your group also gets a low battery alert before your phone dies.
Can I use it with a group of friends rather than one person?
Yes. Groups are unlimited and each one has its own detail level, so a housemate and a wider friend group can see different amounts of you at the same time.
Do the people I share with have to pay?
No. One subscription covers everyone you invite, with no per-person fee and no cap on how many people are in your groups.
How does the safety check-in work?
Set a window before you head out, from a walk to a flight, and share your location with the people you'd want to know. HeyPolo closes the window automatically when the time's up, so the check-in ends itself instead of you having to remember.
