What is real-time location sharing?
Real-time location sharing is a phone feature that sends your live position to chosen contacts as you move, updating continuously rather than showing a single pinned spot. On HeyPolo, your position refreshes every 5 seconds while sharing is on, and you can set it to end automatically after a set window instead of running until someone remembers to turn it off.
Most people turn it on after one specific worry repeats: a teenager heading home in the dark, a parent living an hour up the highway, a new driver on their first solo trip. Everyone in the group controls their own settings, exact location or general area, which is usually what keeps a family still using it past the first couple of weeks.

Real-time location sharing FAQ
What is the best location sharing app for families?
The one everybody in the household will keep installed. We built HeyPolo so each person controls their own sharing, picks exact location or general area, and can pause without leaving the group. One subscription covers unlimited members, it runs the same on iPhone and Android, and your data is never sold.
Can I share my location in real time without giving my exact address?
Yes. General area sharing shows roughly where you are instead of pinning you to a doorstep, and you can set it separately for every group. Your parents might see the neighborhood while a close friend sees the exact spot. Switch between levels whenever the situation changes.
Is a location sharing app the same as a tracking app?
Search results file them together, though they work differently. Products sold as a tracking app or locator app are designed around the person watching. HeyPolo hands the controls to whoever is sharing, so they set the detail level, the audience, and the stop time, and both sides can see the arrangement.
How do I stop sharing my location on HeyPolo?
Tap private mode and sharing pauses immediately, while you stay in your groups. You can also set a window at the start, for a commute or a night out, and sharing closes itself when the time runs out. Nothing lingers in the background afterward.
Can someone see my location without me knowing?
No. Sharing begins only when you switch it on, and every person you're sharing with is visible to you inside the app. Consent works in both directions on HeyPolo, so nobody appears on a map they didn't agree to join.
How often does my position update, and how accurate is it?
Every 5 seconds while the app is open and you're watching the map, and every 30 seconds when it's closed or minimised. That slower background interval is deliberate, since checking every few seconds around the clock is what drains most location-sharing apps. Accuracy averages around 5 metres, tighter outdoors with a clear view of the sky and looser indoors, underground, or between tall buildings.
How many days of location history are kept?
Seven. You can look back at where someone has been over the past week without leaving live tracking running all day, and after 7 days that data is removed entirely.
Is there an app to know where your family is that works on both iPhone and Android?
HeyPolo does, with matching features across platforms. Mixed-device households are normal, and built-in phone sharing usually stops at the platform boundary. Download it from the App Store or Google Play, create a group, then share the invite code with whoever you'd like in it.
How much battery does it use?
With sharing on in the background, HeyPolo uses roughly what a messaging app left open uses, about 4% a day. That climbs in weak signal areas, where the phone works harder to hold a connection, and drops if you share a general area instead of an exact spot or limit sharing to set time windows.
