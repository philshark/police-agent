Got Questions?
Why do people call GeoZilla a scam?
Across 400+ Trustpilot reviews and BBB complaints, the same story repeats: a trial or low intro price converts into a full charge, users are told no refund is available, and support doesn't respond. HeyPolo has one flat price and a 30-day money-back guarantee.
Does deleting GeoZilla cancel the subscription?
No, and GeoZilla's own support team repeats that as a standing reply on Trustpilot, which tells you how often it catches people out. HeyPolo billing runs through your app store, so cancelling there stops it.
Does GeoZilla sell your location data?
Its policy states data is not sold, but the same policy discloses sharing location and driving-sensor data with third parties to inform and improve third-party advertising, and names an external vendor that receives driving data. HeyPolo never sells or shares your location for advertising.
Is HeyPolo more reliable than GeoZilla?
GeoZilla reviewers frequently report random logouts, tracking that silently stops and delayed locations. HeyPolo is built and maintained by a company whose core product is this app, and support is included on every plan.
How much does HeyPolo cost?
One plan, $3.99/month billed annually ($47.88 upfront) or $5.99 month to month, covering everyone you invite across unlimited groups. No trial trapdoor and no tier upgrades.
Will I lose features by switching from GeoZilla?
The everyday ones come across: live location, place alerts, location history, driving speed monitoring, crash notifications and SOS. You also gain severe weather warnings and general-area sharing.
Can someone see my exact location all the time?
Only if you choose that. HeyPolo lets you set exact, general area or private per person, and change it silently. GeoZilla is exact-location only.
How long does switching take?
Usually under ten minutes: download HeyPolo, create a group, invite your people, set precision, then cancel GeoZilla through your app store, not by deleting the app.
