How accurate and real-time is it?
HeyPolo shows each member's live position on the map and updates as they move, using your phone's GPS. Arrival and departure alerts arrive automatically.
Does it work between iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo works on both, so an iPhone user and an Android user can share location with each other.
Can I control what I share?
Always. Everyone chooses what they share, when, and with whom, and can switch to a private mode.
Who can I share my location with?
Anyone you invite: a partner, friends, roommates or relatives. One plan covers everyone in your group, with no per-person fees.
What if it's not right for me?
