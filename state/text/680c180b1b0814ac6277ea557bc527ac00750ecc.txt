What is a location alert app?
A location alert app tells you when someone you're sharing with reaches or leaves a place you've both flagged, home, work, a friend's house, so you get a notification instead of opening the app to check a map. HeyPolo calls this a place alert. Pick a spot, choose who it applies to, and you'll hear about it the moment something changes there.
It does a different job than watching someone's position move in real time. A place alert only speaks up when something actually happens, an arrival or a departure, and stays quiet the rest of the time. If you want the moving dot as well, that's the real-time location sharing feature, linked further down this page.

When does an arrival alert actually go out?
An arrival alert fires when someone's phone registers that they've reached the place you set, based on their location updating in the background. There's usually a short gap between them physically arriving and the notification landing on your phone, how short depends on their connection and how their phone is checking in at that moment.
The same goes for leaving. HeyPolo doesn't run on a stopwatch, it works off real location updates. If someone's phone loses signal right as they arrive, the alert catches up as soon as it reconnects.
Do place alerts cover people leaving too?
Yes. Place alerts fire on arrivals and departures, so you'll know both when someone gets somewhere and when they head out again, from the same alert you set up once.
Set one for home and you'll hear about the school run leaving in the morning and the arrival back in the afternoon, no separate setup for each direction.
Where do you turn on location alerts?
You turn on a place alert from the person's profile in HeyPolo: pick the place, pick who it's for, and save it. From then on, every arrival or departure at that spot sends a notification, until you turn it off.
Can you get an alert when someone arrives home?
Yes, set a place alert on their home address and HeyPolo notifies you the moment they get there, no need to text and ask or watch the map yourself.
Which family members can you set an arrival alert for?
Anyone in your HeyPolo circle who's shared their location with you, a partner, a parent, a kid old enough to carry a phone. The alert works the same way regardless of who it's for, you're just choosing the person and the place.
Someone who joined your circle on an invite code gets the same arrival alerts as everyone else, they just can't start a group of their own.
Is there an alert for when someone leaves the house?
Yes, a place alert set on home fires on departure as well as arrival, so you'll know when someone heads out, not just when they get back.
Does HeyPolo do safe arrival notifications?
A place alert works well for that: set one on home, a friend's place or wherever someone's headed, and you'll know they got there. It's the same feature, arrival and departure alerts, used for peace of mind after a night out, a solo walk or a long drive.
Will I know when my child gets to school?
You can set a place alert on the school and get notified the moment they arrive, and again when they leave in the afternoon.
HeyPolo doesn't have a way to limit that alert to school hours only. If you only want the school run and not every trip past the building, a timed sharing window (say, 7 to 9am) is the closest workaround right now, not a dedicated schedule feature.

Frequently asked questions
How often do location alerts update?
As often as your phone's location updates in the background, there's no fixed schedule down to the second. Most alerts land within a few minutes of the actual arrival or departure, depending on connection and battery settings.
Can someone set a place alert on me without my knowledge?
No. Alerts only work between people already sharing their location with each other, and that sharing starts with an explicit invite you accept. If it's active, you'll know, there's a persistent indicator whenever sharing is on.
Does it work if my phone is offline?
Your last known position stays visible with a timestamp, and the arrival or departure alert fires as soon as your phone reconnects.
Does this drain my battery?
It shouldn't. Place alerts run on background location checks rather than a constant live stream, which is lighter than keeping a map open and refreshing it yourself.
How long is my alert history kept?
Your location data, including anything tied to a place alert, is processed for 30 days or until you withdraw consent, whichever comes first.
Can I set alerts for a limited time only?
Yes. The sharing window behind an alert can run anywhere from 1 minute up to 24 hours, or until you stop it. Close the window and alerts stop firing for that period too.
Can I set alerts for more than one place?
Yes, there's no limit built into the feature. Set one for home, one for school, one for work, for as many people and places as matter to you.
Does it matter what phone the other person has?
No, place alerts work the same way across iOS, Android and web, so it doesn't matter what anyone in your circle is carrying.
