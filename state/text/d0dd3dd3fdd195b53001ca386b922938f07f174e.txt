Frequently asked questions
What location-sharing features does HeyPolo have?
Real-time location sharing that refreshes every 5 seconds, arrival and departure notifications, low-battery and emergency alerts, unlimited groups, per-person sharing controls with a Private Mode, and 7 days of trip history.
How do I create groups on HeyPolo?
Open the app, tap Groups, and create a new one. Name it, invite the people you want in it, and choose what that group can see. You can make as many groups as you like, and each one has its own sharing settings.
How do I add friends on HeyPolo?
Send them an invite link from the app or let them scan your code. Once they accept, you both choose what you share with each other. People you invite join free, only one person in a family pays.
How is HeyPolo different from other location sharing services?
One subscription covers everyone you invite, sharing is always the sender's call, and location history clears after 7 days. Your data is never sold, and Private Mode turns sharing off completely whenever you want.
Is it possible to stay private while sharing your location?
Yes. You can share a general area instead of an exact pin, share only for a set period, or switch on Private Mode so nothing is shared at all. Every setting is per person and per group.
Is HeyPolo owned by Surfshark?
While HeyPolo is indeed a product developed by the same team behind Surfshark, our award-winning VPN service, it operates as a separate product with its own product development team.
How do I contact HeyPolo customer support?
Message us in live chat inside the app, or email support@heypolo.com. Real people answer, and they'll stay with your question until it's sorted.
How do I delete my HeyPolo account?
Go to Settings, then Account, and choose Delete account. Your profile, groups and location history are removed. If you'd rather we do it for you, email support@heypolo.com.
