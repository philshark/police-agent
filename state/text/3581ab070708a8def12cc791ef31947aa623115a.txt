Location history is a record of where you and your group have been, so you can look back at past routes, confirm arrivals and check how a journey went without asking anyone. In HeyPolo, each person chooses what they share and who sees it, and history deletes itself after 30 days.

Location history FAQ
Is there an app that shows my location history?
Yes. HeyPolo shows the past 30 days of trips for anyone who chose to share them, including routes, arrival times and finished journeys. Each person decides who sees their history and how detailed it is, and older records delete themselves.
What is the difference between a location history tracker and a location sharing app?
They overlap, but the controls can sit with different people. Some apps give them entirely to the account owner. HeyPolo gives them to whoever is sharing, so your teen or your dad chooses their own level of detail for each group, and everyone can see the arrangement.
Do I need a GPS tracker to see location history?
Probably not, if everyone already carries a phone. HeyPolo runs on the phone in their pocket, refreshes every 5 seconds while sharing is on, and shows the last 30 days of routes to the people invited.
How far back does location history go in HeyPolo?
30 days. Older trips are deleted automatically, so there is nothing to clear by hand. Whatever someone shared in that window appears as a route with times, and a stretch of private mode shows as a pause.
Can someone see my location history without me knowing?
No. Sharing starts with your agreement, and you can check what each group can see at any time. Private mode stops your sharing straight away and tells the group you've paused.
What is the best location history app for a family?
The one everyone in the family is happy to use. HeyPolo lets each person choose exact or general-area sharing, pause with private mode and set different visibility for each group, all under one subscription.
How do I stop sharing my location history?
Tap private mode to pause sharing at once, or set a time window before you start so sharing ends by itself. Your group sees the change either way.
How often does my position update, and how accurate is it?
Every 5 seconds while the app is open, and every 30 seconds when it is closed or minimised. The slower background interval keeps battery use low. Accuracy averages around 5 metres, tighter outdoors with a clear view of the sky and looser indoors, underground or between tall buildings.
How do I see my location history in the HeyPolo app?
Open the HeyPolo app and tap the group or person whose history you want to check. Their recent routes, arrival times and saved trips appear on the map view. You can see history only for people who have chosen to share with you.
Is there an iPhone location history app that also works on Android?
Yes. HeyPolo is a location history app for both iPhone and Android. One person subscribes and invites everyone else for free, and each person sees the same shared history whichever phone they use.
Is HeyPolo a family location history app?
Yes. HeyPolo works as a family location history app, showing past routes, arrivals and journeys for everyone in the group. Each person controls what they share and for how long.
Is there an app that shows past locations?
Yes. With their permission, HeyPolo shows where someone has been: past routes on a map, time-stamped stops, and arrival and departure times for saved places. Only people in the same group can see it, and it deletes itself after 30 days.
How do I view the location history of a family member?
Add your family member to a HeyPolo group, then open their profile from the group screen. You will see their recent routes, arrivals and trip summaries for anything they chose to share. They can pause with private mode at any time.
Does it work the same on iPhone and Android?
Yes. Features, performance and access are the same, so everyone in a group sees the same thing whichever phone they carry. Background sharing typically uses about as much battery as a messaging app left open, and less if you share a general area or use time windows.
Can I use my location history to work out where I was on a certain day?
Yes, for the last 30 days. Open your history and look at the day in question to see your routes, stops and arrival times. If someone in your group has shared with you, you can check their history for a given day too. Some people use it to piece together a day for an official report.
