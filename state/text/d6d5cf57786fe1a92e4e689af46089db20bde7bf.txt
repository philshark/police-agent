Got Questions?
Does Find My work with Android phones?
No. Find My is limited to Apple devices, so a family or friend group with even one Android phone can't all be on the same map. HeyPolo works on iOS and Android with the same features on both.
Can I see location history in Find My?
Not for people. Apple's own support forums confirm Find My shows a live position or a last-known position, with no route or timeline. HeyPolo keeps seven days of routes and arrival times, then deletes them.
Does Find My tell my family if I crash?
Crash Detection is a phone-level iOS feature that calls emergency services, it doesn't notify the people you share your location with through Find My. HeyPolo sends crash notifications straight to your group with your location.
Is Find My draining my battery?
Background battery drain from Find My is a long-running complaint on Apple Community and MacRumors, in one reported iOS 18.1 case Apple support described it as expected behaviour. HeyPolo is built to report location efficiently rather than continuously.
Why do I get false Find My notifications?
Chronic false 'left behind' alerts have been reported since iOS 15 and still come up today. HeyPolo's alerts are tied to places and people you set up yourself, and each one can be switched off.
Does HeyPolo have groups like circles?
Yes. Private groups are built in, so family, friends and housemates stay separate, each with their own sharing rules. Find My has no group structure at all.
Find My is free. Why pay for HeyPolo?
You're paying for what Find My doesn't do: Android support, location history, SOS alerts to your group, crash notifications your family actually receives, severe weather warnings and general-area sharing. It's one flat price for everyone you invite.
Can I share a general area instead of my exact location?
Yes, per person, and you can change it without anyone being notified. Find My is exact location only.
