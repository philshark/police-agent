What is crash detection?
Crash detection is a safety feature that uses a phone's motion sensors to recognize the sudden forces of a car accident, things like a hard impact, sudden deceleration, or a rollover. If it detects a crash, the phone checks in with the driver and gives them 60 seconds to confirm they're okay. No response in that window sends an alert, with the driver's location, to every group they're in and their emergency contacts.
It runs automatically in the background, so nobody has to press anything before pulling out of a driveway. That matters most for new drivers on early solo trips, older parents still driving to appointments, and anyone whose route runs through quiet roads at night.

Crash detection FAQ
Is there an app that detects car crashes automatically?
Yes. HeyPolo reads impact forces through sensors already built into a phone, then asks the driver whether they're okay and gives them 60 seconds to respond. Silence after that prompt sends an emergency alert with live location to every group that person belongs to, plus their emergency contacts. Nothing gets launched before a drive, so it works while a handset sits in a cupholder or pocket.
How does an app detect a car crash and notify family?
Car accident detection works through the motion sensors already inside a phone. HeyPolo reads for the sudden forces of a crash, a hard impact, fast deceleration, or a rollover, then gives the driver 60 seconds to confirm they're okay. If nobody responds in that window, HeyPolo sends an alert, with live location, to every group the driver belongs to and their emergency contacts. A car accident detection app does something a phone call can't. It notifies everyone at once, even if the driver can't reach for their phone.
Does crash detection work hands free?
Yes. This accident detection app runs in the background the whole time. Nobody has to open it, tap anything, or remember to switch it on before a drive. The phone can sit in a pocket, a cupholder, or a bag, and the only moment it needs a response is the 60-second check-in after a crash.
What's the best crash detection app for a family?
That depends on what a family actually needs: alerts that reach everyone at once instead of one phone, controls each person can set for themselves, and pricing that doesn't climb every time someone joins. HeyPolo covers all three with one subscription for the whole family and a 30-day money-back guarantee if it doesn't fit.
Can a smartphone detect a car crash without a smartwatch?
Yes. iPhone car accident detection doesn't need a smartwatch. An iPhone or Android phone already carries the motion sensors that pick up a crash's sudden forces, and HeyPolo reads those directly, so there's no extra hardware to buy or keep charged.
Does crash detection mean my family can track me while I drive?
No. Crash detection only sends something if the 60-second check-in goes unanswered after a crash. HeyPolo is a car crash alert app first. Regular location sharing is a separate, per-group choice you control on your own.
Can I turn crash detection off?
Yes. Crash detection switches off per group whenever you want, and turning it back on takes one tap too. Nothing about it is locked in.
How much battery does it use?
Around 4% a day running in the background, in line with how often it needs to check for the sudden forces of a crash.
