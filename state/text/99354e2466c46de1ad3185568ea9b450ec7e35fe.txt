What is crash detection?
Crash detection is a safety feature that uses a phone's motion sensors to recognize the sudden forces of a car accident, things like a hard impact, sudden deceleration, or a rollover. If it detects a crash, the phone checks in with the driver, and if there's no response within a set window, it alerts emergency contacts with the driver's location.
It runs automatically in the background, so nobody has to press anything before pulling out of a driveway. That matters most for new drivers on early solo trips, older parents still driving to appointments, and anyone whose route runs through quiet roads at night.

Crash detection FAQ
Is there an app that detects car crashes automatically?
Yes. HeyPolo reads impact forces through sensors already built into a phone, then asks the driver whether they're okay. Silence after that prompt sends an emergency alert with live location to whichever group that person chose. Nothing gets launched before a drive, so it works while a handset sits in a cupholder or pocket.
How does an app detect a car crash and notify family?
Your phone recognizes the sharp change in motion that a serious collision produces, then waits briefly for a response. When none comes, an SOS crash detection alert goes out with a live map pin to every member of your chosen group at once, rather than to one person who might be asleep. Whoever sees it first can call, drive over, or ring emergency services.
Does crash detection work hands free?
It does. A hands free crash detection app asks nothing of a driver mid-journey. HeyPolo runs quietly in the background, and only speaks up with a prompt after sensing a heavy impact. Clearing a false trigger takes one tap, which means a pothole or a dropped phone won't pull your family out of bed.
What's the best crash detection app for a family?
Pick whichever one everybody will keep installed. HeyPolo gives each person control over their own sharing, covers unlimited invited members on a single subscription, and behaves the same on iPhone and Android. Teenagers and grandparents tend to stay with a crash detection app when they can read their settings themselves and change them freely.
Can a smartphone detect a car crash without a smartwatch?
Yes. A smartphone app that detects a car crash relies on motion sensors inside the handset, so no watch or dash device is required. Keeping your phone charged and its location permission enabled matters more than any extra hardware, which is why low battery alerts sit right beside crash detection in HeyPolo.
Does crash detection mean my family can track me while I drive?
No. Nobody follows your movements unless you've chosen to share them, and crash detection alone stays quiet until an impact registers. HeyPolo lets you show a general area, an exact pin, or nothing at all, group by group, while still sending help if a crash happens.
Can I turn crash detection off?
Any time, in one tap, and per group if that suits you better. Pausing everything through private mode keeps you inside your group without sharing a location. Both sides of any sharing arrangement see current settings, so switching something off holds no surprises.
How much battery does it use?
Running in the background with sharing on, HeyPolo typically uses about as much battery as a messaging app left open, around 4% a day. That climbs in weak signal areas, and drops if you share a general area instead of an exact location, or limit sharing to set time windows.
