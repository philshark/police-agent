Life360
Life360 is one of the biggest names in family location sharing. Launched in 2008, it has built a user base of over 90 million people and a feature set that goes well beyond basic location sharing.
It covers driving behavior, crash detection, roadside assistance, and identity theft protection, depending on the plan. For families who want a single app that handles a lot of ground, it's a strong option.
The app offers a free plan with basic location sharing, and paid tiers that unlock additional features. Users can join or create up to 10 circles in total.
The trade-off for that feature depth is data privacy. Life360's business model has historically included sharing user location data with third-party advertisers — something the company has faced public criticism for.
It has also been subject to data breaches, which is a significant concern when the data in question is your real-time whereabouts.
Some opt-out options exist, but they're inconsistent: opting out on the website doesn't necessarily apply to the app.
Life360's privacy policy runs to nearly 10,000 words and is rated at a college reading level, making it difficult for most users to fully understand what they're agreeing to. What it says, in plain terms: Life360 may use your movement patterns to infer things about you and target you with ads.
If you're comfortable with those trade-offs, Life360 is a capable and feature-rich product. If data privacy is a priority, it's worth reading the fine print carefully before signing up.

FAQ
Is there a better option than Life360?
Yes, but it depends on your priorities. If robust driving monitoring or parental controls are important, Life360 covers those well. But if you'd rather your location data stayed private, HeyPolo is a strong alternative — same core features, no data sold to advertisers, and full control over your own visibility.
Does Life360 sell your data?
Yes, Life360 has shared user location data with third-party advertisers — a practice that attracted widespread media criticism. While opt-outs exist, they don't apply consistently across the app and website.
If data privacy is your concern, it's worth reading the privacy settings carefully before signing up.
Can you tell if someone is watching you on Life360?
No. Life360 doesn't alert you when someone views your location. Members of your circle can check where you are without any notification on your end.
Is it safe to use Life360?
Yes, as an app, Life360 is legitimate and widely used.
The more relevant concern is data privacy. Life360 has acknowledged sharing user location data with third-party advertisers, and opting out isn't straightforward — settings differ between the app and the website.
If you're comfortable with that trade-off, it's a functional product. If not, it's worth looking at alternatives.
What is the most accurate location-sharing app?
Most location-sharing apps, including Life360 and HeyPolo, use GPS for live location tracking — so accuracy is broadly similar across the category.
The bigger differences are in how much control you have over what's shared, and what happens to that data — whether it's shared with third parties, sold to advertisers, or kept private.
Can you pause location sharing without anyone knowing?
On HeyPolo, yes. You can pause or switch to private mode at any time — no notification is sent to your group. Your location simply stops updating. What you share and when is always your call.
