Frequently asked questions
Does everyone I invite have to pay for HeyPolo?
No. One subscription covers your whole crew, and invited members join at no cost. Price holds steady whether five people or fifteen come along, so nobody gets a bill for being on your map and nobody has to split anything.
Can I find my friends between iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo runs on both, and everyone lands on the same shared map regardless of which handset they own. Mixed groups won't need a workaround, a temporary link, or a second app for whoever sits outside your platform.
Is HeyPolo a tracker app?
Searches often use words like tracker or locator, though what happens here is different. Nobody watches anyone. Each person shares what they choose, with whom, for a set stretch of time, and both sides see that arrangement openly in the app.
Will sharing my location drain my battery?
Live position uses your phone's location service while sharing runs, so a live map costs some power on any app. Shorter windows and general-area mode cut that down. Low-battery alerts also warn your group before a handset gives out.
Can a friend stop sharing with me whenever they want?
Always, and that's deliberate. Anyone can switch to private mode, drop to general area, or let a window expire on its own. You'll see when someone pauses, so a blank space on your map never leaves you guessing what happened.
How many friends can join one group?
As many as you like. Groups are unlimited and so is membership, which suits a big festival crew as easily as two flatmates. Separate groups keep circles apart, each with its own visibility setting for every person inside.
Do my friends need to be saved in my contacts?
No. Create a group, copy the invite code at the bottom of your screen, then send that code however you normally message people. Whoever joins picks their own sharing level before anything becomes visible.
Is location sharing with friends private?
It can be, when the app puts sharers in charge. Choose general area or exact position, choose who sees each level, and choose the hours. Where you go travels to your chosen contacts, encrypted, and reaches nobody else.
