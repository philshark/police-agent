Frequently asked questions
Can my teen turn off location sharing?
Yes. Private mode pauses sharing whenever they choose, and the rest of the group sees that it's paused. Nothing hides behind a silent setting, which is a large part of why teenagers keep the app installed past week one.
Is HeyPolo a teen tracker?
Searches for a teen tracker do land people here, and it's a fair question to ask up front. HeyPolo is a location-sharing app, so your teenager chooses what you see, group by group, and pauses it at will. You'll know when they get somewhere, and they'll know you can see it.
How is this different from a teen monitoring app?
A teen monitoring app is usually built around the parent alone, with the settings living on one side. Here both people see the same choices, and a teenager can share their general area rather than an exact pin while still letting you know they arrived.
What should I look for in a teen tracking app for a new driver?
Something that tells you they got there. HeyPolo pairs arrival alerts with speed alerts, and neither one reports back on how your teenager handled a roundabout. Reassurance about the destination does the job without turning a drive into a review.
Should I track my teenager?
It's a fair question, and the honest answer depends on how you do it. Tracking someone without their knowledge tends to break trust faster than it builds safety, and teenagers usually find out anyway. HeyPolo asks your teenager to agree to what they share, so you still get the reassurance of knowing they're okay, without the app becoming something you're hiding from them.
How can I track my teenager?
Add HeyPolo and invite your teenager to your family group. They agree to what they share and for how long, and you'll get arrival alerts plus their location, exact or general area, depending on what the two of you settled on. Nothing runs quietly in the background, because they can see every share and pause it whenever they want.
What's the best teen tracking app for families?
The one your teenager keeps installed. An app they agreed to survives past week one, and one they didn't tends to get disabled or deleted. HeyPolo puts the sharing settings in their hands, covers everyone you invite on a single plan, and never sells your family's location data.
Do I need a teen location tracker or GPS device as well?
No, the phone your teenager already carries covers it. There's no extra hardware to buy, no teen location tracker to charge, and nothing to lose at the bottom of a school bag.
Does everyone I invite have to pay?
One person subscribes and everybody they invite joins free. Adding a second teen, a partner, or a grandparent leaves the price where it was, which is how HeyPolo pricing works no matter how large your family gets.
How many people can join one group?
As many as you like, across as many groups as suit your family. Plenty of households run one group for immediate family and another for cousins or grandparents, with different visibility settings on each.
Does everyone need to download the app?
Yes, each person installs HeyPolo on iPhone or Android and joins with the group code you send them. Setup takes a few minutes, and your teenager sets their own sharing preferences during it.
At what age should family location sharing stop?
No single age suits every household. Many parents ease off gradually, moving from exact location to general area, then to sharing only on certain evenings. Because your teen holds the controls, that shift can happen without a negotiation.
