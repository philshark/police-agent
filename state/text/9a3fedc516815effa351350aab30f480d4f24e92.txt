What driving data is tracked?
Trips, speed, harsh braking, rapid acceleration, harsh cornering and phone use while driving — combined into a Driving Safety Score.
Who can see the driving reports?
Only the family members the driver chooses to share with. Your data is never sold or shared with insurers.
Does it affect my insurance?
No. HeyPolo isn't connected to any insurer, so nothing you share can change your premium.
