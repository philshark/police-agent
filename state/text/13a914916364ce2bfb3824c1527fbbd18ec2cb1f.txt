HeyPolo vs Find My: Which Location-Sharing App Is Better?
HeyPolo and Apple’s Find My both make it easy for you to share your location with family and friends. The two services have a lot in common, but where they diverge is where things get interesting. If you’re looking for the best location-sharing app to stay connected, this guide breaks down how they compare in terms of features, pricing, and more.

HeyPolo
HeyPolo is a privacy-focused location-sharing app that puts consent and flexibility first. Both you and your loved ones stay in control over what you share, when you share it, and who can see it.
The app is designed for everyday life. Whether you’re checking in on an elderly family member, coordinating plans with your partner, or keeping up with your teen’s routine, HeyPolo helps you stay connected in a way that feels respectful and reassuring rather than intrusive.
Built by the same privacy-focused team behind Surfshark, HeyPolo follows a strict no-data-selling policy. Meaning, your geolocation, trip history, and other data is never sold or monetized without your consent. Plus, all location data is encrypted during transit and at rest.
HeyPolo also works across both iPhone and Android, unlike platform-locked services like Find My. This means everyone in your family and friend groups gets the same experience and features, no matter what device or brand they use. So platform compatibility doesn’t get in the way of location sharing.

Pros:
Privacy-first: built around user consent, you have complete control over who can see your location, how precise it is, and for how long;
Cross-platform compatibility: HeyPolo works on both iPhone and Android, making it suitable for mixed-device families and groups;
Customizable groups: create as many groups as you need and customize settings for each one;
Safety features: features like low-battery notifications and emergency sharing mean you can step in quickly when needed;
No data selling: HeyPolo doesn’t share, monetize, or sell your location data without your consent;
No per-person fee: one plan covers family members, partners, friends, or anyone else you invite, with no extra charges.

No free plan: HeyPolo doesn’t offer a free plan beyond its 7-day free trial, but it does come with a 30-day money-back guarantee;
No native integration: you’ll need to download the HeyPolo app before you can start sharing your location;
No hardware tracking support: HeyPolo is app-based only, which means you need a smartphone to share your location.

It also comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can try it out risk-free. You can download the app on both the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Find My
Find My is Apple’s built-in location-sharing and device-finding service. It serves a dual purpose. You can share your location with family and friends, and you can also find your Apple devices such as iPhones, AirPods, and AirTags, all from a single app.
Part of what makes Find My so popular is that it comes pre-installed on Apple devices and is tightly integrated into the Apple ecosystem. It’s a convenient option if you want a simple way to stay connected with people you care about without installing a third-party app.
Getting started is pretty straightforward, too. You just sign in with your Apple ID, turn on Find My, and you can start sharing your location.
Where Find My falls short is its cross-platform support. It loses much of its functionality in mixed-device groups.
You can access Find My through any browser, including on Android devices, and use it to find Apple devices linked to your account. But that’s pretty much where it stops. You can’t use Find My to find phones, tablets, or other devices running Android. Plus, you don’t get access to the full set of features available on Apple devices.
Put simply, Find My works best if everyone in your circle uses Apple devices, which isn’t typical for most households or groups.
Pros:
All-in-one solution: works for people and belongings, combining location sharing, device finding, and item tracking in a single app;
Built into Apple devices: no need to install additional apps, making it easy for older family members, young children, or less tech-savvy users to share their location;
Seamless Apple integration: works across iPhones, iPads, AirTags, and other devices within the Apple ecosystem;
Lightweight performance: since it’s built into iOS rather than a standalone app, Find My runs smoothly in the background and uses battery efficiently;
Completely free: core location-sharing and device-finding features are included with compatible Apple devices and Apple IDs at no additional cost.
Cons:
Limited cross-platform support: Android users can only use Find My through a browser and can’t access the full range of features available to iPhone users;
No Android device support: you can’t use Find My to find Android phones or tablets;
Fewer core features: Find My focuses on essential location sharing and device finding, but skips family, safety, and advanced features you often get in dedicated apps.
Find My is included for free on Apple devices. You don’t have to pay subscription fees for location sharing, device finding, or item tracking. However, you’ll need Apple hardware to access all the features, and accessories like AirTags are sold separately.

Find My offers helpful features for sharing your location with your loved ones. That said, most of them are only available on Apple devices.
Here are some of the highlights:

Control sharing: choose whether to share your location indefinitely or only for a limited period of time;
Arrival and departure alerts: get alerts when your child leaves for school, your parent arrives home, or your partner gets to the office;
Precision Finding: pinpoint your contact’s location down to a few feet, direction of travel, and speed on certain iOS;
Precision Finding notifications: receive a notification whenever a loved one uses Precision Finding to locate you;
Messages integration: view your contact’s current shared location directly within the Messages app on supported iPhone and iPad models.

Which app is right for you?
When it comes to HeyPolo vs Find My, the best app depends on what you actually need and how you plan to use it.
If you’re fully immersed in the Apple ecosystem with Apple-only family and friend groups, Find My can be a good option for simple location sharing, especially if you prefer not to install a third-party app. You might also consider Find My appealing if you want location sharing and device tracking in a single built-in service.
For families, couples, and friends who want a location-sharing app that doesn’t compromise on privacy, HeyPolo is the more practical choice. Its cross-platform compatibility also makes it better suited for mixed-device groups, where people are using both iPhone and Android.

FAQ
Is there a better tracking app than Find My?
Yes, but it really depends on what you need. Find My’s Precision Finding feature is a helpful tool that can guide you to within a few feet of your contact’s location by showing direction and distance.
However, if you’re looking for more flexibility and control, HeyPolo might be the better option. Its precision controls let you choose between sharing your exact location when you need accuracy and an approximate location when you want more privacy.
What is better than Find My iPhone?
It depends on your needs and preferences. Find My works well if you want free location sharing built into Apple devices. It’s a simple, useful solution for Apple-only households or groups.
However, if you need cross-platform compatibility, you may find HeyPolo one of the strongest Find My app alternatives. It works on both iPhone and Android, making it a more flexible option for mixed-device families.
Can you tell if someone is viewing your location on an iPhone?
No, iPhone doesn’t notify you when someone is viewing your location.
If you’re using Find My, you can control who can see your location. However, Apple doesn’t keep a log or send alerts when someone checks it.
