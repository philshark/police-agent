How does crash detection work?
HeyPolo uses your phone's motion sensors during a drive to recognise the signs of a serious impact, then alerts your family with your location.
What if it triggers by mistake?
You can cancel the alert with one tap. If there's no response within 60 seconds, your family is alerted with your exact location.
Who gets notified in a crash?
The members of your HeyPolo family group, with your exact location on the map.
Does it call emergency services?
No. HeyPolo alerts your family with your exact location. In a life-threatening emergency, always call 911.
