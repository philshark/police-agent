Questions you probably have
Can my parents see me when I've turned sharing off?
No. Private mode means no location goes out at all, and your group can see that private mode is on. There isn't a hidden setting that keeps sharing when you've said stop.
Do I have to share my exact location?
No. General area shows the neighborhood or part of town you're in without the exact street. Lots of people use that as their normal setting and switch to exact only when it's useful, like meeting up somewhere new.
Can it see my messages, apps, or screen time?
No. HeyPolo is a location and safety app. It doesn't read messages, count screen time, or block apps, and there's no version of it that does.
What does the SOS button actually do?
It sends your exact location to your group and lets them know you need help, so they can call you or come to you. You choose when to use it.
Will it drain my battery?
Updates only go out while you're sharing, so it stays modest day to day. Your group also gets a low battery alert when your phone is close to empty, which explains a pin that stopped moving.
Is my location sold to anyone?
No. Your location data isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. The privacy policy is written to be readable, not to be skipped.
Can I set it up so sharing turns off automatically?
Yes, that's the point of timed sharing. Set a window when you start, and sharing ends on its own so you don't have to remember to switch it off.
Do I need my own account?
Yes, HeyPolo goes on your own phone and you join the group with a code. Your settings are yours, not something someone else configures for you.
