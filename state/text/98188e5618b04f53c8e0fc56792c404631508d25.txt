Will a kids safety app feel like spying to your kid?
A kids safety app can land badly if it feels like something done to your kid instead of with them. HeyPolo only shows you what they've chosen to share: their exact spot, a general area, or nothing at all in private mode.
They don't have to share all day either. They can turn it on for the walk home or a trip across town, and it switches off on its own when they're done. Whatever setting they choose shows up for your side too, so nothing's happening behind their back, or yours.
When they do share, you get an alert once they've arrived somewhere, which cuts down on the "are you there yet" texts. HeyPolo never sells their location to anyone, and it doesn't read their messages, track their screen time, or lock them out of apps.

Questions you probably have
Can I see my kid when they've turned sharing off?
No. Private mode means no location goes out at all, and you'll see that private mode is on. There's no hidden setting that keeps sharing after they've said stop, and no way around it from your side either.
Does my kid have to share their exact location?
No. General area shows the neighborhood or part of town they're in, without the exact street. A lot of families use that as the everyday setting and switch to exact only when it's useful, like meeting up somewhere new.
Can I see my kid's messages, apps, or screen time?
No. HeyPolo is a location and safety app. It doesn't read messages, count screen time, or block apps, and there's no version of it that does.
What does the SOS button actually do?
It sends your kid's exact location to everyone in their group and lets you know they need help, so you can call or go to them. They choose when to use it, and it works even if they're set to private or general area.
Will it drain my kid's battery?
Updates only go out while they're sharing, so it stays modest day to day. You'll also get a low battery alert when their phone is close to empty, which explains a pin that stopped moving.
Is my kid's location sold to anyone?
No. Their location data isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. The privacy policy is written to be readable, not to be skipped.
Can my kid set sharing to turn off automatically?
Yes, that's the point of timed sharing. They set a window, anywhere from one minute up to 24 hours, and it ends on its own so neither of you has to remember to switch it off.
Does my kid need their own account?
Yes. HeyPolo goes on their own phone, and they join your group with a code. Their settings are theirs, not something you configure for them from your side.
How do I track my kid's location with HeyPolo?
Add HeyPolo, start a group, and send your kid an invite code. Once they join and pick what to share, exact location, general area, or a timed window, you'll see it update on your side, and they'll see that you can see it. Nothing shows up that they haven't agreed to first.
Do I need a separate phone tracker or GPS device?
No. A lot of what turns up under phone tracker for kids or child location tracker app means a separate download or a piece of hardware to charge and keep track of. HeyPolo runs on the phone your kid already carries, whether that's Android or a child location iPhone app you had in mind, nothing extra to buy or lose.
