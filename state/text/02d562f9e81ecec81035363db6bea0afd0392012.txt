Search for an app to let your family know you got home and most of them still expect you to remember to open one mid-shift. Shift hours don't run on a schedule, so there's no reliable moment for a check-in text to land. When one doesn't show up, whoever's waiting doesn't know if it's a late handover, a dead phone, or just a shift running long, and twelve hours in, remembering to type anything is usually the first thing you'll forget.
HeyPolo sends the alert for you. The moment you get home, or reach work, whoever you've shared with just knows. No message required.

Frequently asked questions
Can my employer use this to track me?
No. HeyPolo is a personal app: you install it, you choose your group, and there's no employer account, staff roster, or workplace dashboard anywhere in it. Only the people you invite can see anything.
Is HeyPolo a check-in app for shift workers?
Yes. HeyPolo works as a check-in app for shift workers whose hours change week to week, sending an arrival alert automatically instead of asking you to remember a text. You still get one-tap SOS and low battery alerts in the same app, so it covers more than the check-in itself.
Do arrival alerts work in the middle of the night?
Yes, they're not tied to a schedule. Whoever's in your group gets the notification when you reach home or work, whether that's at 6am or 2am, so nobody has to stay up to confirm it.
Do I have to share my location for the whole shift?
No. Most people share for the commute and give it an end time, so it closes itself once they're in. Private mode is always available if you'd rather share nothing at all.
What does the SOS button do on a late walk home?
One tap sends your exact location to your group and tells them you need help, so they can ring you or head to you. It's a shortcut to the people who already care, not an emergency dispatch service.
Is there a safety app for working late alone?
Yes. HeyPolo works as a safety app for working late alone, whether that's the walk across an empty car park or the wait at a night stop. Turn on location sharing for that stretch, and the SOS button is a thumb away if you need your group to call you.
How do low battery alerts help?
They warn your group before your phone dies, so a pin that stopped moving has an obvious explanation. That's usually the difference between a shrug and a sleepless hour.
Does everyone waiting up have to pay?
No. One subscription covers everyone you invite, with no per-person fee and no cap on group size, so a partner, a parent, and a housemate can all be included.
Will it run down my battery on a long shift?
Updates only go out while you're sharing, and most people share for the commute rather than the whole shift, which keeps daily use modest.
Is my location data sold?
No. It isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest.
