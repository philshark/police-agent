Frequently asked questions
How quickly can I get this working?
A few minutes. HeyPolo installs on the phone your loved one already carries, both of you join the same group with a code, and you add home as the first place. There's no device to order and nothing to wait for.
Does HeyPolo have a wandering alert system?
HeyPolo isn't built as a dedicated wandering alert app, and we'd rather say that upfront than oversell it. What it does have is arrival and place alerts, so you're notified when someone reaches a place you've set, such as home or a day centre, plus a live map you can check any time in between.
Does this mean tracking someone constantly?
It doesn't have to, and mostly it shouldn't. General area shows the part of town someone's in without following every step, and many families use that as the normal setting, moving to exact location only when there's a reason.
What if the person I care for finds it intrusive?
Everyone in the group can see what's being shared and by whom, so the arrangement is out in the open and can be adjusted together. Setting it up as something you both agreed to, with precision they're comfortable with, is what keeps it in place.
How does SOS work for them?
One tap sends their exact location to the group and tells everyone help is needed, so you can call them or go to them. It reaches family, not a call centre, and it isn't an emergency dispatch service.
What happens if their phone battery dies?
Your group gets a low battery alert before it does, which usually explains a pin that stopped moving. Keeping a charger in a coat pocket or bag is the small habit that pairs with it.
Can several relatives be in the group?
Yes, with no cap and no per-person fee. Siblings, an in-law, or a neighbour with a key can all be included for one subscription, so the load is shared.
Is the location data sold or shared?
No. It isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. HeyPolo comes from the team behind Surfshark, and the privacy policy is written in plain language.
