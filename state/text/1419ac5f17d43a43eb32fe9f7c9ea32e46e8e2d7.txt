What is group location sharing?
Group location sharing means you can share your location with several people at once within specific groups or circles. This is super helpful for families, friend groups, travel buddies, and teams to stay coordinated and safe. HeyPolo offers a simple, privacy-first way to do just that.
Most group location sharing apps put all the control in the hands of the person who created the group. HeyPolo gives every member of the group control over their own sharing — what they share, how precisely, and for how long. That’s the difference between a surveillance tool and something people actually want to use.

Frequently asked questions
How many groups can I create in HeyPolo?
Unlimited. You can create as many groups as you need — one for your family, one for your friends, one for a trip, one for colleagues. There are no restrictions on the number of groups per account.
Is there a limit to how many people can be in a group?
No. You can add as many people as you want to any group. And because everyone you invite joins free, there's no cost implication to adding more members.
Can I be in multiple groups at the same time?
Yes. You can be an active member of multiple groups simultaneously, with different sharing settings in each one. You might share your exact location with your family group while sharing only a general area with a friends group — your preferences are set independently for each.
Can I control what I share in a group?
Yes — and this is one of the things that makes HeyPolo different from most group location sharing apps. Every member of a group controls their own visibility independently. You can share your exact location, a general area, or go completely private — and you can change your settings any time without affecting anyone else in the group.
Does everyone in the group need to pay?
No. One person subscribes and everyone else joins for free. There are no per-person fees regardless of how many people are in your groups.
