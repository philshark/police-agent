What is group location sharing?
Group location sharing puts everyone in a small invited circle on one shared map. You might also hear it called a family map app, a family circle app or location sharing circles. Each member decides whether the group sees their exact location, a general area or nothing at all.
A group on HeyPolo begins when one person creates it and passes round a join code. From there, each member picks their own visibility for that circle: exact location refreshing every 5 seconds, a general area instead of a precise pin, a time window that closes itself, or private mode. Nobody lands on the map by accident, and everybody can see who's sharing what.
Parents of phone-age kids lean on this. So do adult children checking in on a parent who lives alone, households with a new driver, and friends splitting up at a festival.

Group location sharing FAQ
What is the best group location app for families?
HeyPolo is our pick, and we'd say so even on a page we didn't write. Every member controls their own sharing, which is what keeps a family circle installed past the first month. Exact location or general area, per-group settings, private mode, and one subscription covering everybody invited. Available on iPhone and Android.
How do I create a location sharing group?
Open the app, tap the group icon at the top of your home screen, then select Create group. Name the circle, choose your role, and adjust your visibility preferences for it. Copy the code at the bottom of that screen and send it to family or friends. Later, tap Add people inside any circle to invite more.
What is the difference between a group tracking app and group location sharing?
A group tracking app and group location sharing solve for different people. A tracking app is usually built around the person watching, while sharing puts the choice with the person being seen. On HeyPolo, a group works as checking in: you decide who sees you, how precisely, and when it ends.
Is a family map app the same as group location sharing?
Yes. Family map app, family circle app, location sharing circles and shared location map app all describe a group of invited people on one map, where each person controls what they share.
Is there an app to see where everyone is on one map?
Yes. Open HeyPolo and every group you belong to shows on one map, each person's dot updating every 5 seconds while they have the app open. You're not hunting through separate messages or apps to find one person; everyone who's chosen to share for that circle is already there. Switch between your family group and a friend group without leaving the map.
Is there an app to track multiple family members?
Yes. One group can hold the whole family, and each member chooses their own sharing setting, so everyone is on the map by choice.
Does HeyPolo work as a family map app on iPhone?
Yes. HeyPolo works on iPhone and Android, so everyone in the family can join whichever phone they use.
How do I share my location with friends?
Create a group and share the join code. Each friend chooses what to share, including exact location for a set window of time.
