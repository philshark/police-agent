About HeyPolo
HeyPolo is a privacy-first location sharing app built around one idea: staying connected should feel like care, not control.
Most location apps track you always and sell your data. HeyPolo does neither. Every person controls their own sharing, what they share, how precisely, and for how long. When the sharing window ends, it stops automatically. No ghost tracking. No data selling. No exceptions.
HeyPolo is built by the team behind Surfshark, protecting the privacy of millions of users across 100+ countries.
