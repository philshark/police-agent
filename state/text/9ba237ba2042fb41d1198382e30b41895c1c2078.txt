Frequently asked questions
What is HeyPolo?
HeyPolo is a family safety and location sharing app for iPhone and Android. Families use it to see each other on a live map and get alerts when someone arrives home, at school or anywhere else that matters. It also has SOS alerts, crash detection and a Driving Safety Score for new drivers. Each person decides what they share and who can see it.
Is HeyPolo free?
HeyPolo is free to download and comes with a 7-day free trial. After the trial, you choose a monthly or annual subscription to keep using it. One subscription covers your whole family, however many people you add. You can see current prices on our pricing page.
Does HeyPolo work on iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo works on iPhones running iOS 17 or later and Android phones running Android 10 or later. Family members on different phones join the same group and see each other on one map.
Does HeyPolo sell my location data?
No. HeyPolo doesn't sell your location data to advertisers, insurers or data brokers. Sharing is based on consent, and you can switch to Private mode at any time to stop sharing your location completely.
What features does HeyPolo have?
HeyPolo includes real-time location sharing, arrival and departure alerts, location history, SOS alerts, crash detection, a Driving Safety Score, speed and phone use insights, low battery alerts, dangerous weather alerts, a compass for finding family nearby, and calls that ring through silent mode or Do Not Disturb.
Is HeyPolo owned by Surfshark?
No. HeyPolo is its own company, HeyPolo Inc., founded in 2025. The app was built by the team behind Surfshark, the VPN trusted by millions of people, and runs as a separate product with its own team.
