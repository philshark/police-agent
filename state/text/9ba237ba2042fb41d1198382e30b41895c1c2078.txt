Frequently asked questions
What is the best family safety app?
The best family safety app depends on what your family needs. HeyPolo combines real-time location sharing, arrival alerts, SOS and teen driver speed monitoring — with privacy controls that put every family member in control of what they share. One plan covers your whole family with no per-person fees.
Is HeyPolo free?
HeyPolo runs on a single subscription that covers your whole family — there are no per-person fees. One plan keeps everyone connected, so the cost never grows as your family does.
Does HeyPolo work on both iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo is available on both iPhone and Android, so everyone in your family can join no matter which phone they carry.
Does HeyPolo sell my location data?
Never. Your family's location is never for sale — no advertisers, no insurers, no data brokers. Not now, not ever. HeyPolo is built on consent, not surveillance.
How is HeyPolo different from Life360?
Unlike always-on tracking apps, HeyPolo is built on consent rather than surveillance. Every family member controls what they share, when, and with whom — and your location data is never sold. You stay connected without constant monitoring.
What features does HeyPolo include?
HeyPolo includes real-time location sharing, arrival alerts, SOS and teen driver speed monitoring — with privacy controls that put every family member in control of what, when, and with whom they share.
