Got Questions?
What happened to Zenly?
Snap acquired Zenly in 2017 and shut it down in early 2023 with under two months' notice, while downloads were at an all-time high. Millions of friend groups lost their shared map with no migration path.
Is there an app like Zenly still running?
HeyPolo covers what most people used Zenly for: a live shared map with friends, private groups and arrival alerts. It adds SOS, crash notifications and severe weather warnings, and it isn't a feature inside a social network.
Could HeyPolo disappear the way Zenly did?
Nobody can promise a company's future, but the setup matters: Zenly was a side project inside Snap, while HeyPolo is built by Surfshark, an established privacy company where this app is the product rather than a growth experiment.
Can I get my data out?
Yes, any time, and location history clears itself after seven days regardless. You don't have to catch a shutdown window to keep control of it.
Is HeyPolo social like Zenly was?
No feed, no public map, no stories. It's private groups you choose, with sharing that you can set to exact, general area or off, per person.
Does HeyPolo sell location data?
No. Your location and movement data is never sold to advertisers, data brokers or insurers, and analytics partners never receive your location.
What does it cost?
One flat plan, $3.99/month billed annually ($47.88 upfront) or $5.99 month to month, covering unlimited members across unlimited groups.
Does it work on both iPhone and Android?
Yes, on both, with the same features on each, so a mixed friend group all sees the same map.
