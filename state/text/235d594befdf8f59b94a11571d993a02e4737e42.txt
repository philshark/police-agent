Got Questions?
Does Life360 sell your location data?
Life360 has shared user location data with third parties, including precise location sold to data brokers as documented by investigative reporting in 2021, which led to a proposed class action in 2023. HeyPolo's position is simple: your location and movement data is never sold, monetised or shared with advertisers, brokers or insurers.
Is there a better option than Life360?
It depends on your priorities. If you want insurance-style extras like roadside assistance and identity theft cover, Life360 goes wider. If you want the everyday essentials, live location, alerts, history, SOS, crash and weather notifications, without your data being sold, HeyPolo is the stronger choice.
Will I lose features by switching from Life360?
The features most people actually use come across: real-time location sharing, location-based notifications, location history, car crash notifications, SOS alerts, severe weather warnings, private groups and driving speed monitoring. What you leave behind are Life360's insurance add-ons.
How much does HeyPolo cost compared with Life360?
HeyPolo is a single plan at $3.99/month billed annually ($47.88 upfront), or $5.99 month to month, and it covers your entire group. Life360's paid tiers start around $7.99/month and are generally priced per member, so a family on a higher tier adds up quickly.
Can someone check my location without me knowing?
On Life360, yes, circle members can view your location with no notification. On HeyPolo, sharing is consent-based and you choose your own precision: exact position, general area, or private.
How long does switching take?
Usually under ten minutes. Download HeyPolo, create a group, invite the people you share with, set each person's precision, and turn Life360 off once everyone has joined.
Does everyone in my family need to pay?
No. One person subscribes and everyone they invite joins free, across unlimited groups. There are no per-seat charges.
Is HeyPolo a parental monitoring app?
No. HeyPolo is built on mutual, consent-based sharing rather than one-sided tracking. Everyone decides what they share and when, which is usually what makes it work with teenagers.
Who is behind HeyPolo?
HeyPolo is built by the team behind Surfshark, an established consumer privacy and security company, and its privacy commitments are written into a policy rated at an 8th-grade reading level versus Life360's college-level policy.
