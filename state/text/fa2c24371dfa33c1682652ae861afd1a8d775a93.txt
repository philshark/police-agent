What happens when I press SOS?
Your family group instantly gets an alert that you need help, with your live location on the map so they can find you.
Who gets notified in an emergency?
The members of your HeyPolo family group — the people you chose to share with.
Does it work if I can't speak?
Yes. SOS is a single tap — no call or typing needed, and your location is sent automatically.
Is this a replacement for 911?
No. HeyPolo alerts your family. In a life-threatening emergency always call 911.
