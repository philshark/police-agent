Bark vs HeyPolo questions
How much does Bark cost per month?
Bark's own pricing lists Premium at $14 a month or $99 a year, and Bark Jr at $5 a month or $49 a year. Several reviewers report a higher iPhone price once the Bark Sync add-on is included. HeyPolo is $3.99/month billed annually or $11.99 month to month.
Is Bark available outside the US?
Bark's FAQ says the app is available in the United States (including U.S. territories), South Africa and Australia, and Bark Phone and Bark Watch only in the US. HeyPolo is available worldwide on iPhone and Android.
Does Bark work on iPhone?
Yes, with limits. Reviewers report that full monitoring on iPhone relies on the Bark Sync charger, which scans when the phone is plugged in rather than in real time.
Can Bark read my child's text messages?
Bark's AI scans texts, email and supported apps, then alerts parents only when it finds a problem. HeyPolo doesn't read messages or monitor apps at all.
Can I use HeyPolo and Bark together?
Yes. They don't overlap much. Bark watches content, and HeyPolo handles location, arrival alerts, crash notifications and SOS.
Does HeyPolo monitor messages or social media?
No. HeyPolo is location sharing built on consent. It has no parental controls and isn't designed for one-sided monitoring.
What's the best Bark alternative for location sharing?
If location is what you need, HeyPolo covers live location, place alerts, SOS, crash and severe weather alerts for the whole family, at $47.88 a year against Bark Premium's $99.
