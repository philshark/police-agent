Got Questions?
Is iSharing free version any good?
It works, but reviewers on the App Store describe the free tier as heavily ad-supported, with popups and store redirects arriving within minutes of opening it, and some history features only unlocking after you watch an ad. HeyPolo has no free ad tier and no ads at all.
Which iSharing features are behind a paywall?
Battery alerts and inactivity alerts need Silver or higher, and high-speed driving alerts are Platinum-only. Google Play's own listing marks low battery alerts, driving alerts and SOS sound recording as Premium. On HeyPolo, every feature is on the single plan.
Does iSharing share your location data?
iSharing's privacy policy states it does not sell personal data, but it also discloses sharing location data with third parties for advertising, research and analytics purposes. HeyPolo never sells your location and never shares it with advertisers, brokers or insurers.
Why do iSharing alerts fire when nobody has moved?
Arrival and departure alerts repeating incorrectly is a recurring theme in Google Play and App Store reviews, including alerts firing while both people were in the same room. HeyPolo's place alerts are unlimited on every plan and can be turned off per place and per person.
Can I get a refund if it isn't for me?
HeyPolo comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee on a single flat price. Several iSharing reviewers report being refused a refund after an annual purchase did not work as they expected.
Does HeyPolo have automatic crash detection?
Yes, and it is included rather than sold as an upgrade. If your phone senses a crash, the people in your group are notified with your location. iSharing has no automatic crash detection at any tier.
Can I share only a general area instead of my exact spot?
Yes. HeyPolo lets you pick exact position, general area, or private, per person, and switch any time without notifying anyone. iSharing is exact location only.
How long does switching from iSharing take?
Usually under ten minutes. Download HeyPolo, create a group, invite the people you share with, set each person's precision, then cancel iSharing once everyone has joined.
