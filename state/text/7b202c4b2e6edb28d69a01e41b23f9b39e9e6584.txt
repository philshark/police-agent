What is real-time location sharing?
Real-time location sharing lets the people you choose see exactly where you are, right now — not an hour ago, not an approximation.
With HeyPolo, you decide who sees your location, how precisely, and for how long. When the time is up, sharing stops automatically. No ghost tracking, no forgetting to turn it off.

Frequently asked questions
Can I share my location in real time?
Yes. HeyPolo shows the people you choose exactly where you are, with your location refreshing every 5 seconds. You stay in control of who can see you, how precisely, and for how long.
Are real-time location and live location the same thing?
They describe the same idea — your current position updating continuously, rather than a single pin dropped an hour ago. HeyPolo updates in real time, so the people you share with always see where you are right now.
How do I share my real-time location permanently?
When you start sharing in HeyPolo, you can keep it on for as long as you like, or set a time window so it stops on its own. There's no hidden tracking running in the background — you decide when sharing starts and ends.
What is the best app for real-time location sharing?
HeyPolo is built around sharing, not surveillance. Everyone controls their own location, one subscription covers everyone you invite, and it works identically on iPhone and Android — making it a simple, private choice for families and friends.
How do I stop real-time location sharing on HeyPolo?
Stop any time with a single tap, or set a timer so sharing ends automatically. When the time is up, sharing stops on its own — no ghost tracking and nothing left running without you knowing.
