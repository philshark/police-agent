Real-time location sharing FAQ
Can I share my location in real time?
Yes. HeyPolo is a live location sharing app, so the people you choose can see where you are, with your location refreshing every 5 seconds while the app is open. You decide who sees you, how precisely and for how long. Keep sharing on indefinitely, or set a time window and it stops on its own.
Is a live location sharing app the same as real-time location sharing?
Yes. A live location app and real-time location sharing describe the same thing: your position updating continuously, not a single pin dropped an hour ago.
What is the best live location sharing app for families?
The best app to share location is the one everybody in the household keeps installed. HeyPolo lets each person control their own sharing, pick exact location or a general area, and pause without leaving the group. One subscription covers everyone you invite, it works the same on iPhone and Android, and your data is never sold.
Is there a cross platform location sharing app that works between iPhone and Android?
Yes. HeyPolo is a cross platform location sharing app with the same features on iPhone and Android, so mixed-device households can share with each other. Download it from the App Store or Google Play, create a group, then send the invite code to whoever you want in it.
Can I share my location in real time without giving my exact address?
Yes. General area sharing shows roughly where you are instead of pinning you to a doorstep, and you can set it separately for every group. Your parents might see the neighborhood while a close friend sees the exact spot. Switch between levels whenever the situation changes.
How do I stop sharing my location on HeyPolo?
Tap private mode and sharing pauses straight away while you stay in your groups. You can also set a window at the start, such as a commute or a night out, and sharing ends on its own when the time runs out.
Is a location sharing app the same as a tracking app?
In HeyPolo, the person sharing sets the detail level, the audience and the stop time, and both sides can see the arrangement. Apps sold as tracking or locator apps tend to put the controls with whoever is looking.
Can someone see my location without me knowing?
No. Sharing begins only when you switch it on, and every person you're sharing with is visible to you inside the app. Consent works in both directions on HeyPolo, so nobody appears on a map they didn't agree to join.
How often does my position update, and how accurate is it?
Every 5 seconds while the app is open and you're watching the map, and every 30 seconds when it's closed or minimised. That slower background interval is deliberate, since checking every few seconds around the clock is what drains most location-sharing apps. Accuracy averages around 5 metres, tighter outdoors with a clear view of the sky and looser indoors, underground, or between tall buildings.
How much battery does it use?
With sharing on in the background, HeyPolo uses roughly what a messaging app left open uses, about 4% a day. That climbs in weak signal areas, where the phone works harder to hold a connection, and drops if you share a general area instead of an exact spot or limit sharing to set time windows.
How many days of location history are kept?
Seven. You can look back at where someone has been over the past week without leaving live tracking running all day, and after 7 days that data is removed entirely.
How much does HeyPolo cost?
Plans start at $7.99 a month billed annually, or $19.99 month to month, and VAT may apply. One person pays and everyone invited joins free, so the cost holds steady as more relatives arrive. There's a 30-day money-back guarantee, and you can cancel whenever you want.
