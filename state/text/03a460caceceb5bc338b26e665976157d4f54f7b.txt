Frequently asked questions
How can I monitor my teenager's driving?
Add HeyPolo and set the places you care about: school, practice, home. You'll get an arrival alert automatically, and speed alerts you can review together once a week, so you're finding out how the drive went instead of tracking it in real time.
Does HeyPolo have crash detection?
We're not claiming that here. What's live today is arrival alerts, speed alerts, and a one-tap SOS that sends an exact location to your group.
How does HeyPolo compare with Life360 for driving, and which is the best app to track teen driving?
Life360's paid tiers have a more built-out driving package. HeyPolo offers speed alerts without selling location data, precision control over what's shared, and one flat family price instead of a per-tier upgrade. Which one's best depends on whether you want more driving detail or fewer strings attached to your data.
Won't speed monitoring feel like being spied on?
It can, if it turns into a running commentary on every trip. Speed alerts work better as a weekly look you take together, not a live feed you check every time they're out.
Can my teen turn sharing off?
Yes. Private mode pauses their sharing while keeping them in the group, and everyone can see it's paused.
Are new drivers really at higher risk?
New drivers are still building experience behind the wheel, which is the whole reason the first year gets more attention than the tenth.
Do arrival alerts need any effort from my teen?
No. Once the places are set, the notification goes out on its own when they get there.
Is my teen's driving data sold to insurers or advertisers?
No. Location and driving data isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
Is there an extra charge for driving features?
No. There are no tiers. Speed alerts, arrival alerts, and SOS are all in the single plan.
