An app to share location with your partner, made for mismatched schedules
The simplest way to describe this is an app to share location with your partner without turning it into a whole conversation. Most of what couples use it for is dull in a good way, whether to start dinner, whether the train got in, whether it's worth leaving for the airport yet. It replaces the running commentary of 'leaving now', 'on the M4', 'twenty minutes' with something either of you can glance at.
When one of you travels for work it earns its keep faster. You can see a flight landed without waiting up for the text, and the person who's away doesn't have to narrate the day across time zones to stay reachable.
It's mutual by default. You both share, you both see, and either of you can show less or stop entirely whenever you want, so it can stay on all week or just cover the drive home. Nothing about your setup is hidden from the other person, which is what makes it easy to leave on.

Frequently asked questions
Isn't this just a tracking app for couples?
People often search for a tracking app for couples, expecting something that watches quietly. HeyPolo is built so it can't work that way. Sharing goes both ways, either person can stop it, and each of you can always see what the other is currently sharing. If someone wanted a covert monitoring tool, this isn't it.
Does sharing have to be on all the time?
No. Timed sharing is the setting most couples live on: share for this drive, this flight or this night out, and it ends automatically. There's no requirement to leave it running.
Can I share less detail without switching off completely?
Yes. General area shows the part of town someone's in rather than an exact street. Plenty of couples keep that as their normal setting and switch to exact only when it's genuinely useful.
What happens if one of us goes private?
Sharing pauses and the other person can see that private mode is on. Nothing goes out and nothing is logged in the background, and there's no notification framed as a warning about it.
Is the SOS button useful for us?
Often, yes, especially for a solo trip or a late walk to the car. One tap sends your exact location to your group so your partner can call you or come to you.
Do we both need to pay?
No. One subscription covers everyone you invite, with no per-person fee and no cap on group size, so a couple pays the same as a large household.
Is our location data sold?
No. It isn't sold, monetized, or shared with advertisers or data brokers, and it's encrypted in transit and at rest. The privacy policy is written in plain language, at roughly an eighth-grade reading level.
Can we keep separate groups for family and friends?
Yes, and each group carries its own level of detail. A partner can see a street while a group chat for a weekend away sees a general area.
